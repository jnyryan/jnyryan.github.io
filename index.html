How I lead

What I believe

  1. Fix the pipes first

    If the data coming in is unreliable, no amount of AI on top will save it. The boring work is the leverage.

  2. Build capabilities, not one-off apps

    Search, data and intelligence should be services that every product and agent can reuse.

  3. Leaders should still build

    The best architecture decisions come from people who still write code. Today that means working alongside AI agents every day.

  4. Grow teams that run themselves

    Remove the blockers, focus on the metrics that matter to the business, and grow leaders who grow their own teams.

About

An engineer who became a leader, and never stopped building

I’ve spent more than 25 years building and scaling data-intensive systems, the last 17 of them at GLG, the world’s leading expert network, where I grew from principal engineer to VP Engineering.

Before that I was a SQL architect and team manager at Microsoft, built systems for financial services in Dublin and for Australia’s financial regulator in Sydney, ran my own consultancy, and was a senior engineer at Dell.

Today my work sits where AI meets real-world data: search that understands intent, data platforms that AI can trust, and engineering teams spread across continents. AI isn’t new to me: my undergraduate thesis in 1998 was an AI engine for analysing MRI scans.

Outside work I’m always building something: robots, VR, speech recognition and, lately, a growing set of AI agent skills. More on GitHub →

Full bio

John Ryan is Vice President of Engineering, Data & Match Intelligence at GLG (Gerson Lehrman Group), the world’s leading expert network. Based in Dublin, he leads the engineering teams responsible for expert matching, data infrastructure, third-party data integration and AI-driven discovery across a platform serving billions of documents.

John has more than 25 years of experience building and scaling data-intensive systems. He joined GLG in 2009 as a Principal Software Engineer. From 2016 he led AI Search and Recommendations as Director of Engineering, heading a global team across the Americas and Europe that brought large language models and natural language processing to how GLG finds experts. Before GLG he was a Senior Software Engineer at Dell, and built systems for Susquehanna International Group and Pioneer Investments. He was a System Architect at the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority in Sydney, and a SQL Architect and team manager at Microsoft. He also founded his own consultancy, Rayn Computing.

John holds a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Software Engineering from TU Dublin, where his thesis was an AI engine for analysing MRI scans, and a master’s degree from Dublin City University. He is open to speaking and advisory roles in AI, search and data.
