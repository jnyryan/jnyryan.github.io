About

An engineer who became a leader, and never stopped building

I’m John Ryan, VP Engineering, Data & Match Intelligence at GLG (Gerson Lehrman Group), the world’s leading expert network. I lead the teams responsible for expert matching, data infrastructure, third-party data integration and AI-driven discovery across a platform serving billions of documents.

I’ve spent more than 25 years building and scaling data-intensive systems. I started as a SQL architect and team manager at Microsoft, built systems for financial services in Dublin and for Australia’s financial regulator in Sydney, ran my own consultancy, and was a senior engineer at Dell before joining GLG in 2009.

At GLG I’ve moved from principal engineer, to leading AI search and recommendations for nearly a decade, to leading engineering for data and matching. My work spans data infrastructure, third-party data integration, AI-driven discovery, and large-scale search that blends semantic AI with keyword precision.

AI isn’t new to me: my undergraduate thesis in 1998 was an AI engine for analysing MRI scans. I still write code every week, and I’m open to speaking, panels and advisory roles on AI, search and data leadership.

How I lead

Architecting teams, systems and strategy

Teams

Create an environment where engineers can innovate and ship quickly: remove the blockers, focus on the metrics that matter to the business, and grow teams until they run themselves.

Systems

Fix the pipes first. If the data coming in is unreliable, no amount of AI on top will save it. And stay hands-on: the best architecture decisions come from people who still build.

Strategy

Understand the business, its customers and what drives it. Engineers sit closest to the data, so I encourage everyone to run with ideas that grow revenue, cut cost or move the metrics.

Full bio

John Ryan is Vice President of Engineering, Data & Match Intelligence at GLG (Gerson Lehrman Group), the world’s leading expert network. Based in Dublin, he leads the engineering teams responsible for expert matching, data infrastructure, third-party data integration and AI-driven discovery across a platform serving billions of documents.

John has more than 25 years of experience building and scaling data-intensive systems. He joined GLG in 2009 as a Principal Software Engineer. From 2016 he led AI Search and Recommendations as Director of Engineering, heading a global team across the Americas and Europe that brought large language models and natural language processing to how GLG finds experts. Before GLG he was a Senior Software Engineer at Dell, and built systems for Susquehanna International Group and Pioneer Investments. He was a System Architect at the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority in Sydney, and a SQL Architect and team manager at Microsoft. He also founded his own consultancy, Rayn Computing.

John holds a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Software Engineering from TU Dublin, where his thesis was an AI engine for analysing MRI scans, and a master’s degree from Dublin City University. He is open to speaking and advisory roles in AI, search and data.
